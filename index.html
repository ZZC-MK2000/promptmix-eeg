PromptMix-EEG

Brain (EEG) → frozen MusicGen via mixture weights over 12 fixed genre prompts. Every clip is a native sample of the untouched generator; the EEG only picks the style and the blend.

FAQ

Why is there no stimulus audio?

The stimuli are excerpts of commercially released songs, so only spectrograms are shown; every playable file is generated. Raw EEG is not published either.

Is the generated clip a reconstruction of what was heard?

No. It matches the heard material at genre level (+0.103) and that advantage disappears within the same genre (−0.005) — the adapter only chooses among 12 style prompts. Song identity is Part 1's job (88.2%).

Why keep the generator frozen?

Fine-tuning scored 7,404 vs 1,389 on the texture metric, and freezing keeps every output inside the generator's own distribution by construction.

Why not interpolate between two prompts?

39% of the 66 pairs go off-manifold: crackle instead of a blend.

What is still missing?

A human listening test (MOS), and coverage is relative to the trivial baseline rather than absolute — the library does not saturate the real-window distribution.

Limitations

  • Small corpus (14 songs, 68 subjects); genre labels skew toward the dominant style, so κ — not accuracy — is the honest number.
  • The margin over the FMA pool measures pull toward this corpus, not song identity: the stimuli are Bollywood/film music, the pool is mostly Western genres.
  • Interpolation being off-manifold makes the "mixture" effectively near-single-prompt, or waveform blending.
  • No metric replaces listening; MOS has not been run.
  • Conditioning is genre-level by design (12 style prompts), so song-level identity is out of reach for the generation arm.
